GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

When will the beta be available?+

We're preparing the first external beta. We haven't announced a date yet. The waitlist will be used to send invitations once the product is ready for testing.

Which platforms will VODhit support?+

The first beta is planned for macOS. Other platforms may be considered after feedback from early users.

Do I have to connect my YouTube account?+

No. VODhit can work with supported exports from YouTube Studio. Some data and refresh features may require an authorized YouTube API connection.

Where are notes and project data stored?+

Project notes are stored locally in Markdown files. The app also supports local backups. API-connected features use authorization and may access YouTube data according to the selected permissions.

Is VODhit an AI tool that guarantees growth?+

No. VODhit provides analytics, rankings, and comparisons to support your own decisions. Changes in performance are not proof that a specific edit caused them.

How much will it cost?+

Pricing has not been announced. Early access will help us understand how creators use the product and what they value most.
